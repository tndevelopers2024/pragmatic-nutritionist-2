Frequently asked questions
01
Why is child nutrition important?
Proper nutrition is essential for a child's growth, development, cognitive function, immunity, and overall health. It sets the foundation for lifelong wellness.
02
Who offers child nutrition plans online in Chennai?
Pragmatic Nutrition, led by Meenu Balaji, is one of India's most established paediatric nutrition practices offering online consultations from Chennai. With over 14 years of experience, Meenu works with children aged 2 to 18 across concerns including growth and weight, picky eating, frequent illness, teen nutrition, and micronutrient deficiencies common in Indian children, such as iron, Vitamin D, and B12.
Every plan is built around Indian food and regional vegetables, making recommendations practical for real Indian families and school schedules.
Online consultations are available for families across India and internationally, including the UK, Australia, Canada, and New Zealand.
For children with ADHD requiring nutrition support specifically around focus and behaviour, we offer a dedicated ADHD nutrition programme separately.
03
Who is the best child nutritionist in India?
The best child nutritionist in India is one who specialises exclusively in paediatric and adolescent nutrition, builds plans around Indian food, and has deep experience across the full range of childhood nutrition concerns — growth, picky eating, immunity, teen nutrition, and micronutrient deficiencies specific to Indian children.
Meenu Balaji of Pragmatic Nutrition is one of India's most experienced paediatric nutritionists, with over 14 years working with children aged 2 to 18 across India and internationally. She offers online consultations across India and in person in Chennai.
04
Can a nutritionist help with picky eating in children?
Yes, and picky eating is one of the most common reasons parents consult a child nutritionist. A specialist can identify whether the picky eating is sensory, behavioural, routine-based, or linked to an underlying nutritional gap, and build a gradual, practical strategy to expand what your child will eat without pressure or force.
At Pragmatic Nutrition, picky eating support is built into every child's nutrition plan as a default, using Indian foods your child already knows as the starting point.
05
My child is underweight, can a nutritionist help?
Yes. Underweight in children can be caused by insufficient caloric intake, poor appetite, picky eating, micronutrient deficiencies, digestive issues, or a combination of these. A paediatric nutritionist assesses the root cause and builds a plan to address it through food — calorie-dense Indian meals, appetite-supporting strategies, and micronutrient correction where needed.
At Pragmatic Nutrition, underweight and growth concerns are among the most frequently addressed issues, and every plan is built around foods the child will actually eat rather than generic high-calorie protocols.
06
Can I get online consultation outside India?
Yes. Meenu consults entirely online with families across India, Chennai, Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Coimbatore, and beyond, as well as with Indian families in the UK, Australia, Canada, New Zealand, and Ireland. All programs are delivered online via video consultation and WhatsApp support.
