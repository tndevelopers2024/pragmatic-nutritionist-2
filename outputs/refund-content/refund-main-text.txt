Under exceptional circumstances, your Nutrition plan can be paused once and resumed at a later date, with a maximum duration of 15 days for pausing.
To request a pause or cancellation please contact us via email on meenu@pragmaticnutritionist, stating your full name, registered email address and reason for pausing/cancelling.
